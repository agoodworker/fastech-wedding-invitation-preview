Our Story

Not a fairytale — a family dinner, a lot of chai, and a question asked properly.

Spring 2021 — The Family Dinner, Lahore

We met at a family dinner

Ayesha's aunt and Hamza's mother had been planning the seating for weeks. Ayesha arrived late, Hamza had already taken the last good chair, and they spent the whole dinner arguing — politely — about who made better biryani. Neither won. Both stayed till the tea went cold.

2021–2023 — The Chai Friendship

Chai turned into a habit

What started as one dinner became Sunday chai, then study sessions, then long drives around Gulberg and MM Alam Road with no destination at all. Ayesha still says Hamza talks too much; Hamza still says Ayesha interrupts too well. The friendship came first, and it never left.

December 2025 — The Proposal

He asked, in front of both families

Hamza did not do a surprise in a restaurant. He waited for a family dawat, stood up after dinner with both mothers watching, and asked Ayesha properly, in front of everyone who mattered. She said yes before he had finished the sentence — and then made him finish it anyway, for the video.

14 February 2027 — Today, With You

Now we want you in the room

The Barat is on Valentine's Day, which Hamza insists was his idea and Ayesha insists was the venue's only free Saturday. Either way: 14 February 2027, Pearl Continental Hotel, Lahore — and the only thing missing is you.

Getting to Lahore

Allama Iqbal International Airport (LHE) is the nearest airport. Allow roughly 30–45 minutes by car to the hotel area in normal traffic, longer at rush hour — book your ride in advance for the Barat evening.

Parking & Shuttle

Valet parking is available at the Barat venue. There is no arranged shuttle between hotels, so please plan a ride for each evening; ride-hailing works well in Lahore, but book early on 14 February.
